Hi, I’m **Nikhilesh Jannu**.

I’m a software architect at **Salesforce** who enjoys building practical systems and understanding how things work under the hood. I use this site as a place to **learn in public**, document ideas, and share what I’m experimenting with.

Most of my work and writing lives at the intersection of:
- Software engineering and system design
- Applied AI and LLMs
- Building tools, workflows, and small projects

I build and experiment under **OpenNJ**, which is my personal space for exploring ideas, shipping projects, and writing openly about the process — including what works and what doesn’t.

I’m inspired by the Stanford GSB motto — *change lives, change organizations, change the world* — and I’m currently enrolled in the **Stanford LEAD** program to deepen my understanding of leadership, strategy, and innovation.

### Why this site exists
This site is intentionally simple. It’s not a portfolio polished for perfection — it’s a living notebook:
- Things I’m learning
- Problems I’m trying to solve
- Projects I’m building or breaking
- Opinions that may evolve over time

### Outside the code
I’m curious by default and enjoy breaking complex ideas into simple explanations. I’m currently working on a side project focused on helping kids learn programming in a modern, intuitive way. If that resonates with you, I’d love to build it together.
